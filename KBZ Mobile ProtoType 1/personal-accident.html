Personal Accident

Protection

Financial support against accidental risks during the policy term.

Sum Insured

5 – 200 lakhs

Term

3 / 6 / 12 mo

Premium

0.7%

0.707% high risk

Payment

Lump-sum

Who should take this policy?

Any individual aged (16) to (65) can take this policy.

Things that are assured

Death
Total Permanent Disability
Injuries and loss of limbs
Hospitalization
Being unable to work

Exclusion Clauses

Suicide, self-inflicted injuries, war-related incidents, and intoxication-related accidents are excluded.

Who can purchase

Individuals aged 16 to 65 years, salaried or self-employed, can purchase this policy.

Benefits

Lump-sum payout, hospitalization coverage, disability benefits, and family financial security.

Endorsements

Policy modifications, nominee changes, and sum insured adjustments can be endorsed.

How to purchase

Tap Get quote to calculate premium, then continue to e-Application.

How to claim

Submit claim form with supporting documents within 30 days of the incident. Processing typically within 15 working days.
