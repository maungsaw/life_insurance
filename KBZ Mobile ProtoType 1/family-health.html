Family Health

Health

Healthcare for your loved ones — hospitalization and treatment costs covered for the whole family.

Sum Insured

5 – 50 lakhs

Term

1 yr, renewable

Premium

From 45,000 MMK/yr

Payment

Cashless / reimbursement

Who should take this policy?

Families wanting hospitalization cover for members aged (1) to (65) under a single plan.

Things that are assured

Hospitalization costs
Pre & post hospitalization care
Day-care procedures
Family floater coverage

Exclusion Clauses

Pre-existing conditions within the first 12 months, cosmetic treatment, and non-prescribed alternative therapies are excluded.

Who can purchase

Any policyholder can enroll immediate family members — spouse, children, and parents — under one plan.

Benefits

Cashless treatment at partner hospitals, annual health check-up, and no-claim discount on renewal.

Endorsements

Adding or removing a family member, and sum insured upgrades, can be endorsed at renewal.

How to purchase

Tap Get quote to calculate premium for your family size, then continue to e-Application.

How to claim

Show the health card at partner hospitals for cashless treatment, or submit bills for reimbursement within 30 days of discharge.
